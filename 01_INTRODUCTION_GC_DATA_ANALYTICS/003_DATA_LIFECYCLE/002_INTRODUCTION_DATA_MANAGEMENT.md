# Introduction to Data Management

## Data Management

- The process of establishing and communicating a clear plan for collecting, storing and using data.
- In some organizations, the data management plan may also be referred to as data governance.

**Importance of data management:**

- Collaboration
- Data security 
- Scalability

## Aspect of Data Management Plan

- Access - user's level of access
- Data types
- Storage
- Archives

## Retention Policy 

- An outline of how data is archived and deleted

## Data-Collection Policy

- An outline that creates rules around how data is collected and what resources may be used in the process. 

## Archival Policy 

- An outline of where and how data is stored once an analysis project is completed. 

## Deletion Policy 

- An outline of when and how data is permanently destroyed.
