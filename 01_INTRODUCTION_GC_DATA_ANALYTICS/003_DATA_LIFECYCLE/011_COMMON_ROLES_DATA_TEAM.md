# Common Roles on a Data Team

## Data analyst

- A professional who performs data analysis workflows, including reporting, manipulating, calculating, and reporting on business data.

Tasks in data cycle:

- Analyzing and reviewing databases
- Using scripting languages
- Creating visualizations
- Presenting findings 

## Data engineer

- A professional who transforms data into a useful format for analysis and gives it a reliable infrastructure. 
- The design, create, manage, migrate, and troubleshoot databases used by applications to store and retrieve data.
- Much of their work involves developing the structure the team uses to collect, store, and analyze the data.
- It's also the one who creates the data pipelines

> [!Data pipeline]
A series of processes that transform data from different sources to a destination for storage and analysis. 

## Data scientists

- Analyze data, do statistical analysis, and usually build and train machine learning models.
- Works primarily in data research, identifying business questions, collecting data from multiple resources, organizing it, and finding answers. 

## Data Architects

- A professional who collaborates with data analysts, data scientists, and data engineers to design the infrastructure of a database.
- Helps their team to plan the overall data lifecycle of a solution.
