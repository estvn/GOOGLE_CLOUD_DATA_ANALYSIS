# Safety and Privacy in the Cloud

## Data Privacy 

- Preserving a data subject's information any time a data transaction occurs.
- Data must to be used honestly, transparently and fairly.

## Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

- Data that is unique to an individual and therefore can be used to distinguish one person from another.

Examples of PII:

- Email address
- Mailing address
- Phone number
- Precise location
- Birthday 
- Full name

## Protected Health Information (PHI)

- Health data that can identify an individual, like information about patient demographics, mental or physical health diagnoses or treatments, and payment records related to healthcare.

## General Data Protection Regulations (GDPR)

- Privacy legislation for organizations in Europe.
- Regulates personal data
- Reporting requirements

## Audit

- A formal examination of how users are accessing data in order to ensure safe and appropriate access while identifying and solving any data concerns 

## Security Key 

- An authentication method that uses a physical digital signature or key, to verify user's identity before accessing specific resources. 

## Encryption 

- The process of encoding information
