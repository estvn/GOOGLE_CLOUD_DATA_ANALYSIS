# Introduction to Data Retention Policies

## Data Retention 

- The collection, storage, and management of data

## Retention policy considerations:

- Internal needs 
- Industry regulations
- Laws

## Data retention process

- Scheduling for saving and deleting data
- Rules for keeping data safe
- Processes for destroying data
- Guidelines for data breaches/incumplimientos

## Other Guidelines

- Format of the data
- Whether the organization archives or destroys data
- Who has authority to make decisions about deletions

## Regulations

- Sarbanes-Oxley Act
- Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)
- Health Insurance and Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
