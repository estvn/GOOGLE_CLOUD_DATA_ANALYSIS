# Benefits of the Cloud Computing

## **Accesibility**

- Access and manage data, software, storage, and cloud infrastructure. 
## **Scalability**

- Easily expand or upgrade computing resources to meet changing needs.
## **Cost Savings** 

- Only pay for the computing resources used.
## Measured Service 

- A service that charges users only for what they use, based on the number of transactions, the storage volume, and the amount of data transferred.
## Security

- Systems, data and computing resources are protected from theft, damage, loss, and unauthorized use.
## Efficiency

- Immediate access to new and upgraded applications.
## Freeing Resources

- Users can focus on more value-added tasks. 
## Managed Service

- The act of third-party provider taking care of the ongoing maintenance, management, and support of an organization's cloud infrastructure and applications.
